Questions

FAQ

Why are prices in dollars if I pay in Bitcoin?

Dollar prices stay stable while BTC moves. At checkout we convert your total at the rate shown on the page and give you the exact BTC amount to send. Send that amount within 30 minutes so the rate still holds.

What happens if I send the wrong amount?

If you underpay, we contact you for the difference before shipping. If you overpay by more than $5, we refund the extra in BTC to an address you give us.

Can I pay with Lightning, ETH or USDT?

Not yet. We accept on-chain Bitcoin (BTC) only. Do not send any other coin or token to our address, because it cannot be recovered.

How do sizes run?

Tees and hoodies run true to size with a relaxed fit. Jackets are cut to layer over a hoodie, so size down if you prefer a close fit. High-tops follow US men's sizing; women should order 1.5 sizes down.

What is your returns policy?

Return unworn items in original packaging within 30 days of delivery. Refunds are paid in BTC at the dollar value of the item. Engraved and made-to-order jewelry is final sale.

How long until my order ships?

Within 1 business day of your payment reaching 2 confirmations, which usually takes 20–40 minutes after you send it.
